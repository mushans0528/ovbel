# 02 - 双语页面文案

## 产品名

- EN: **Classical Wrapped V-Belt**
- ZH: **普通包布 V 带**

## Eyebrow

- EN: Power transmission drives
- ZH: 动力传动带

## Summary

- EN: Classical wrapped V-belt in standard profiles A/B/C/D/E/F/Z, rubber construction, 40° angle, for general industrial drives.
- ZH: 标准 A/B/C/D/E/F/Z 型普通包布 V 带，橡胶结构、40° 带轮槽角，适用于一般工业传动。

## Description

- EN: OVBEL classical wrapped V-belts are designed for general-purpose industrial power transmission. The belt body is built from a rubber compound and wrapped with fabric for wear resistance and grip on pulley grooves. Standard profiles cover A, B, C, D, E, F and Z sections, with a 40° groove angle and black or customised finish. These belts suit fractional-horsepower to medium-duty drives where reliable frictional power transfer, low stretch and easy maintenance are required.
- ZH: OVBEL 普通包布 V 带用于常规工业动力传动。带体采用橡胶配方，外包织物以提高与带轮槽的抓附力和耐磨性。标准截面覆盖 A、B、C、D、E、F、Z 七种型号，带轮槽角 40°，常规为黑色，也可按需定制颜色。适用于从小功率到中等负载的摩擦传动场合，具有传动可靠、伸长小、安装维护方便等特点。

## Feature Heading

- EN: Wrapped rubber construction for reliable frictional drives.
- ZH: 包布橡胶结构，摩擦传动可靠。

## Features

- EN:
  1. Rubber compound body with wrapped fabric cover for groove grip and wear resistance
  2. Standard classical profiles A / B / C / D / E / F / Z available
  3. 40° pulley groove angle for common industrial drives
  4. Black as standard; customised colour on request
  5. Suitable for OEM/ODM programs
  6. Low-stretch tensile member for stable drive geometry
  7. Easy installation and tensioning compared with chain or gear drives
- ZH:
  1. 橡胶带体配外包织物，增强带轮槽抓附力与耐磨性
  2. 提供标准普通 V 带 A / B / C / D / E / F / Z 七种截面
  3. 40° 带轮槽角，适配常见工业传动结构
  4. 常规黑色，可定制颜色
  5. 支持 OEM/ODM 合作
  6. 低伸长抗拉层，保持传动几何稳定
  7. 相比链条或齿轮传动，安装与张紧更简便

## Applications

- EN: Agricultural machinery and irrigation pumps; Air compressors and blowers; Industrial fans and ventilation systems; Machine tools and workshop equipment; Conveyor and processing-line drives; Generators and small power units; Water pumps and cooling systems.
- ZH: 农机及灌溉水泵；空压机与鼓风机；工业风机与通风设备；机床及车间设备；输送机与产线传动；发电机及小型动力机组；水泵与冷却系统。

## Precautions

- EN: Store in a cool, dry, ventilated place away from direct sunlight, heat sources, oils and chemicals. Avoid kinking, folding or hanging on small pegs that can deform the belt. Check pulley groove alignment and tension during installation; re-tension after the first run-in period. Replace matched belt sets together to maintain even load sharing.
- ZH: 存放于阴凉、干燥、通风处，避免阳光直射、热源、油类及化学品接触。避免折弯、打结或悬挂于小直径桩上，以防带体变形。安装时检查带轮槽对中与张紧力，运行初期后重新张紧。多根组配使用时，建议成套同时更换，保证负载均匀。

## Inquiry Checklist

- EN: Required profile (A, B, C, D, E, F or Z); Inside length or datum length, or pulley centre distance and speed ratio; Quantity and any matched-set requirement; Colour preference (black / customised); Application or driven machine type; Operating environment (dust, oil, temperature).
- ZH: 所需截面型号（A、B、C、D、E、F 或 Z）；内周长或基准长度，或带轮中心距与速比；数量及是否需要配组使用；颜色偏好（黑色 / 定制）；应用场合或被驱动设备类型；使用环境（粉尘、油污、温度）。

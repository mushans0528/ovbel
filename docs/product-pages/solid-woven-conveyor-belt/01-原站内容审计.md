## 已确认事实（可直接使用）
1. **产品名称**: Solid Woven Conveyor Belt（整芯输送带）
2. **所属分类**: Conveyor Belt（输送带）
3. **产品类型**: 
   - PVC solid woven conveyor belt（PVC 全塑整芯输送带）
   - Nitrile covered PVG type（PVG 橡胶面整芯输送带）
4. **结构与材料**: 合成纤维（涤纶、尼龙）和棉纤维加捻编织成整体带芯；PVC 型经 PVC 糊料浸渍、PVC 膜涂覆、二次塑化制成；PVG 型在 PVC 基础上挂胶硫化。
5. **适用场景（原站原文）**: Especially suitable for material conveying at inflammable of underground coal mines.
6. **性能特点（原站原文）**: 
   - The fabric is high in strength, with low elongation, light carcass, shock resistant anti-tear and good in trough ability
   - PVC 型：Suitable for applications in dry conditions at a slope angle of no greater than 16 degrees. Cover thickness can be from 0.5 to 4 mm.
   - PVG 型：Suitable for applications at a slope angle of less than 20 degrees, thickness can be 1 to 8 mm. Resistance to wetness, slip, low temperature and wear resistance of the belt.
7. **标准**: MT914, BS3289, HG2805
8. **规格表（来自页面图片）**: 见 [02-双语页面文案.md](./02-双语页面文案.md) 规格表。

# 02 — 双语页面文案

## 英文文案

- **eyebrow**: Synchronous toothed drive
- **summary**: Rubber timing belt with a neoprene body, glass-fibre cords and nylon tooth facing for slip-free synchronous power transmission.
- **description**: A timing (synchronous) belt transmits power through the positive engagement of belt teeth with matching pulley grooves, so there is no slip and the transmission ratio stays accurate. The belt body uses neoprene (CR) as the main elastomer and is reinforced with glass-fibre cords for low stretch and stable pitch; the tooth surface is faced with nylon 66 elasticity cloth to reduce wear and running noise. The result is a clean, lubrication-free drive with high efficiency, low noise and a wide usable power range.
- **featureHeading**: Positive tooth engagement — no slip, accurate ratio.
- **features**:
  - Neoprene (CR) body for flexible, durable power transmission
  - Glass-fibre cord reinforcement for low stretch and stable pitch
  - Nylon 66 tooth facing reduces tooth wear and running noise
  - Positive tooth engagement — no slip and an accurate transmission ratio
  - No lubrication required; clean, low-noise operation
  - Resists cracking, ozone, ageing, heat, oil and abrasion
  - Transmission efficiency around 0.98 (reference)
  - Suitable for multi-axis drive arrangements
- **applications**:
  - Textile and weaving machinery
  - Printing and packaging equipment
  - Office automation and business machines
  - Automotive engine timing (camshaft) drives
  - CNC and precision spindle drives
  - General industrial synchronous drives
- **precautions**:
  - Store rolls in a cool, dry, ventilated place away from direct sunlight
  - Keep away from heat sources, ozone-generating equipment and open flames
  - Avoid contact with oils, solvents, acids and alkalis that can attack the rubber
  - Do not kink, twist or store under heavy load; protect the teeth from impact
  - Confirm pulley groove geometry, alignment and tension before installation
- **inquiryChecklist**:
  - Tooth profile / pitch (e.g. MXL, XL, L, H, HTD, STD)
  - Belt length (number of teeth or pitch length)
  - Belt width
  - Material or performance requirement (neoprene body, glass-fibre cord)
  - Operating conditions: speed, transmitted load, temperature
  - Quantity and destination port

## 中文文案

- **eyebrow**: 齿形同步传动
- **summary**: 采用氯丁橡胶带体、玻璃纤维承拉层和尼龙齿面包布的橡胶同步带，实现无滑差同步传动。
- **description**: 同步带依靠带齿与带轮齿槽的啮合传递动力，无滑动、传动比准确。带体以氯丁橡胶（CR）为主弹性体，内嵌玻璃纤维承拉层以控制伸长和节距稳定；齿面覆以尼龙 66 弹性包布，降低磨损与运行噪声。整体形成免润滑、高效率、低噪声且功率适用范围广的传动。
- **featureHeading**: 齿形啮合 — 无滑差，传动比准确。
- **features**:
  - 氯丁橡胶（CR）带体，柔韧耐用
  - 玻璃纤维承拉层，伸长小、节距稳定
  - 尼龙 66 齿面包布，降低齿面磨损与噪声
  - 齿形啮合传动，无滑差、传动比准确
  - 免润滑，运行清洁、低噪声
  - 抗龟裂、耐臭氧、耐老化、耐热、耐油、耐磨
  - 传动效率约 0.98（参考）
  - 适用于多轴传动布置
- **applications**:
  - 纺织与织造机械
  - 印刷与包装设备
  - 办公自动化与商务设备
  - 汽车发动机正时（凸轮轴）传动
  - 数控机床与精密主轴传动
  - 通用工业同步传动
- **precautions**:
  - 卷装存放于阴凉、干燥、通风处，避免阳光直射
  - 远离热源、臭氧发生装置和明火
  - 避免接触可侵蚀橡胶的油类、溶剂、酸碱
  - 禁止弯折、扭曲或重压，保护齿部免受撞击
  - 安装前确认带轮齿形、对中及张紧
- **inquiryChecklist**:
  - 齿形 / 节距（如 MXL、XL、L、H、HTD、STD）
  - 带长（齿数或节线长）
  - 带宽
  - 材料或性能要求（氯丁橡胶带体、玻璃纤维承拉层）
  - 工况：带速、传递载荷、温度
  - 数量与目的港

# 02 — 双语页面文案

## 英文文案

### Eyebrow
Multi-ply fabric carcass conveying

### Title / Name
EP/Nylon Conveyor Belt

### Summary
Multi-ply EP or nylon fabric-reinforced rubber conveyor belt for medium to long-distance, heavy-load bulk material transport.

### Description
The EP/Nylon conveyor belt uses a multi-ply fabric carcass: EP belts are woven with polyester warp and nylon-66 weft, while NN belts use nylon in both warp and weft. The construction provides low elongation, good troughability, water resistance and high adhesion between plies, making it suitable for medium and long-distance heavy-load conveying.

### Application Intro
EP/Nylon conveyor belts are selected for general bulk-material conveying in mining, ports, metallurgy, building materials, chemicals, power and grain handling. Strength grade, ply count and cover type are confirmed against the material, loading and operating conditions before quotation.

### Feature Heading
Multi-ply EP or nylon fabric carcass for reliable bulk conveying.

### Features
1. EP carcass with polyester warp and nylon-66 weft for low elongation and high initial modulus
2. NN carcass with nylon warp and weft for excellent abrasion resistance and fatigue resistance
3. Good troughability and inter-ply adhesion for stable running under load
4. Thin belt body with good shock resistance and flexibility
5. Good water resistance and wet strength for outdoor and humid conditions
6. Suitable for medium, long-distance and heavy-load material transport

### Applications
- Mining and quarrying
- Ports and terminals
- Metallurgical industry
- Building and construction materials
- Chemical engineering
- Power generation
- Grain handling and storage

### Specifications
- Carcass types: EP (polyester warp / nylon-66 weft) and NN (nylon warp / nylon weft)
- EP fabric specs: EP-80 to EP-500; ply thickness 0.8–2.0 mm/P (see technical table)
- NN fabric specs: NN-100 to NN-500; ply thickness 0.7–1.8 mm/P (see technical table)
- Strength range (EP): 160–3000 N/mm depending on fabric spec and ply count
- Strength range (NN): 200–3000 N/mm depending on fabric spec and ply count
- Cover thickness: Upper 1.5–8 mm; lower 0–4.5 mm (see technical table)
- Belt width: 400–2500 mm (confirm per strength grade and roll length)
- Roll length: ≤ 300–500 m; confirmed before quotation

### Model / Series Options
- EP-80 to EP-160 series — Light to medium-duty polyester carcass grades for general conveying.
- EP-200 to EP-500 series — Medium to heavy-duty polyester carcass grades for higher loads and longer centres.
- NN-100 to NN-200 series — Light to medium-duty nylon carcass grades with excellent flexibility.
- NN-250 to NN-500 series — Medium to heavy-duty nylon carcass grades for fatigue-resistant service.

### Storage & Handling
- Store rolls off the ground in a clean, dry, ventilated place away from direct sunlight.
- Keep away from heat sources, ozone-generating equipment and open flames.
- Avoid contact with oils, solvents, acids and alkalis that may attack the rubber.
- Handle with suitable lifting equipment; do not drop, drag or bend sharply.
- Check belt tension, splices and pulley alignment during installation and initial running.

### Inquiry Checklist
- Conveyed material, lump size and abrasiveness
- Carcass type (EP or NN) and fabric spec / strength grade
- Required belt width, length and ply count
- Cover thickness and cover grade (wear / heat / oil / flame-retardant as required)
- Operating environment, temperature and humidity
- Quantity required and destination port

## 中文文案

### 眉标
多层织物芯输送

### 标题 / 名称
EP/尼龙输送带

### 摘要
以多层 EP 或尼龙织物为带芯的橡胶输送带，适用于中远距离、重载散料输送。

### 详情描述
EP/尼龙输送带采用多层织物带芯结构：EP 型经线为聚酯、纬线为尼龙 66；NN 型经线与纬线均为尼龙。该类带芯具有伸长率低、成槽性好、耐水湿、层间粘合强等特点，适用于中远距离重载散料输送。

### 应用说明
EP/尼龙输送带适用于矿山、港口、冶金、建材、化工、电力及粮食等行业的散料输送。强度等级、层数及覆盖胶类型需结合物料、载荷与运行工况在报价前确认。

### 优势标题
EP 或尼龙多层织物带芯，满足通用重载输送需求。

### 优势
1. EP 带芯：经向聚酯、纬向尼龙 66，初始模量高，伸长率低
2. NN 带芯：经向与纬向均为尼龙，耐磨、耐疲劳、柔韧性好
3. 成槽性与层间粘合力良好，负载下运行稳定
4. 带体薄、强度高、抗冲击、柔韧性好
5. 耐水湿性好，适用于户外及潮湿工况
6. 适用于中远距离、重载物料输送

### 应用行业
- 矿山与采石场
- 港口与码头
- 冶金行业
- 建材行业
- 化工行业
- 电力行业
- 粮食装卸与仓储

### 规格参数
- 带芯类型：EP 型（聚酯经向 / 尼龙 66 纬向）与 NN 型（尼龙经向 / 尼龙纬向）
- EP 织物规格：EP-80 至 EP-500；单层厚度 0.8–2.0 mm/P（详见技术图片）
- NN 织物规格：NN-100 至 NN-500；单层厚度 0.7–1.8 mm/P（详见技术图片）
- 强度范围（EP）：160–3000 N/mm，具体取决于织物规格与层数
- 强度范围（NN）：200–3000 N/mm，具体取决于织物规格与层数
- 覆盖胶厚度：上覆盖胶 1.5–8 mm；下覆盖胶 0–4.5 mm（详见技术图片）
- 带宽：400–2500 mm（需按强度等级与卷长确认）
- 卷长：≤ 300–500 m，具体在报价前确认

### 型号 / 系列选择
- EP-80 至 EP-160 系列：轻型至中型聚酯织物芯等级，适用于一般输送工况。
- EP-200 至 EP-500 系列：中型至重型聚酯织物芯等级，适用于更高载荷与较长中心距。
- NN-100 至 NN-200 系列：轻型至中型尼龙织物芯等级，柔韧性好。
- NN-250 至 NN-500 系列：中型至重型尼龙织物芯等级，耐疲劳性能优异。

### 储运须知
- 卷装应离地存放于清洁、干燥、通风处，避免阳光直射。
- 远离热源、臭氧发生源和明火。
- 避免接触油品、溶剂、酸碱等可能侵蚀橡胶的化学品。
- 使用合适吊装设备搬运，禁止抛掷、拖拽和剧烈弯折。
- 安装与初期运行时检查张紧、接头与带轮对中。

### 询盘清单
- 被输送物料、块度及磨蚀性
- 带芯类型（EP 或 NN）及织物规格 / 强度等级
- 所需带宽、带长与层数
- 上下覆盖胶厚度及覆盖胶等级（耐磨/耐热/耐油/阻燃等）
- 运行环境、温度与湿度
- 需求量与目的港

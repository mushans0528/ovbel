# 02 - 双语页面文案

## 英文文案

- **Name**: Conveyor Belt Cover
- **Eyebrow**: Conveyor protection hood
- **Summary**: Corrugated steel hoods that protect outdoor belt conveyors, drives and walkways from weather, dust and debris.
- **Description**: Conveyor belt covers are formed from color-coated corrugated sheet into half-round or arched sections that shield the belt, carried material and head/tail sections from rain, wind-blown dust and falling debris. They are typically supplied in standardized arc lengths and fixed to the conveyor frame or walkway, with overlap joints to allow thermal expansion and assembly access.
- **Application intro**: Belt covers are used on outdoor or partially exposed conveyors where weather protection, dust containment and safe access are required. Length, radius, color and fixing details are customized to the conveyor width and structure; final drawings should be confirmed before quotation.
- **Feature heading**: Weather protection designed around conveyor geometry.
- **Features**:
  1. Corrugated steel construction with color coating
  2. Arched or half-round profile for belt and walkway coverage
  3. Overlapping joints allow expansion and service access
  4. Custom colors and sizes on request
  5. Helps reduce dust emission and material contamination
  6. Suitable for fixed and portable conveyor systems
- **Applications**:
  - Outdoor mining and quarry conveyors
  - Coal and aggregate stockyard belts
  - Cement plant material transport
  - Ports and terminals
  - Recycling and bulk handling systems
- **Specifications**:
  - Product name: Conveyor Belt Cover
  - Material: Color-coated corrugated steel (source page lists "Stainless steel"; confirm actual grade)
  - Usage: Conveyor belt system
  - Color: Customizable
  - Size: Customizable
  - Packing: Carton box or customized
  - Condition: New
- **Precautions**:
  1. Confirm conveyor width, hood radius and total covered length before ordering.
  2. Check frame or walkway fixing points and spacing.
  3. Allow for thermal expansion at overlap joints.
  4. Handle sheet sections carefully to avoid dents and coating damage.
  5. Store in a dry, ventilated area before installation.
- **Inquiry checklist**:
  - Conveyor width and belt speed
  - Required hood radius / profile
  - Total covering length and section length preference
  - Color and coating specification
  - Fixing method (frame-mounted or walkway-mounted)
  - Quantity, packing and destination port
- **SEO**:
  - Title: Conveyor Belt Cover | Weather & Dust Protection Hoods
  - Description: Corrugated steel conveyor belt covers for outdoor material handling systems. Customizable color and size, protects belts and walkways from rain, dust and debris.

## 中文文案

- **Name**: 输送机防雨罩
- **Eyebrow**: 输送机防护罩
- **Summary**: 用于保护户外带式输送机、驱动装置及人行通道免受风雨、粉尘和杂物影响的波纹彩钢罩壳。
- **Description**: 输送机防雨罩由彩涂波纹钢板压制成半圆形或拱形罩壳，覆盖皮带、承载物料及机头机尾区，起到防雨、防风沙、防尘及阻挡坠落杂物的作用。通常按输送机宽度定制半径，以标准弧段供货，通过搭接方式与机架或走道固定，兼顾热胀冷缩与检修开口需求。
- **Application intro**: 防雨罩适用于露天或半露天布置的输送机，需要防护天气、控制扬尘并保障通道安全的场合。长度、半径、颜色及固定方式需按输送机宽度和结构定制，最终图纸请在报价前确认。
- **Feature heading**: 按输送机结构定制的防护方案。
- **Features**:
  1. 彩涂波纹钢板结构
  2. 半圆形/拱形罩壳覆盖皮带与通道
  3. 搭接式接口兼顾热胀冷缩与检修
  4. 颜色与尺寸可定制
  5. 有助于降低粉尘逸散和物料污染
  6. 适用于固定式和移动式输送机系统
- **Applications**:

  - 煤炭与骨料堆场皮带机

  - 港口与码头
  - 回收及散料处理系统
- **Specifications**:
  - 产品名称：输送机防雨罩
  - 材质：彩涂波纹钢板（页面标注不锈钢，需确认实际牌号）
  - 用途：输送机系统
  - 颜色：可定制
  - 尺寸：可定制
  - 包装：纸箱或按客户要求
  - 状态：全新
- **Precautions**:
  1. 订货前确认输送机宽度、罩壳半径及覆盖总长。

# 02 — 双语页面文案

## 英文文案

- **eyebrow**: Sealing and edge protection
- **summary**: Custom-cut EPDM rubber seal strip for waterproof, windproof, dustproof and edge-protective sealing.
- **description**: U-channel and bulb-edge EPDM rubber seal strips supplied to customer dimensions. The profile forms a flexible seal around panels, doors, hatches and sheet edges, with good weather, UV and ozone resistance for indoor and outdoor use.
- **featureHeading**: Flexible EPDM profiles for reliable sealing.
- **features**:
  - EPDM rubber compound with 60–65 Shore A hardness
  - U-channel and bulb-edge profiles for panel and edge sealing
  - Waterproof, windproof, airproof and dustproof sealing
  - Good UV, ozone and weathering resistance
  - Custom sizes and lengths available
  - Suitable for mining equipment, machinery, vehicles and marine applications
- **applications**:
  - Mining and construction machinery cabs and compartments
  - Industrial equipment doors, hatches and access panels
  - Commercial vehicle and truck body sealing
  - Marine and boat deck, window and door sealing
  - General rubber sealing and edge protection
- **precautions**:
  - Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, ozone sources and solvents
  - Avoid sharp bends, kinking and contact with oils that can degrade EPDM
  - Cut profiles with clean tools to maintain edge quality
  - Confirm groove dimensions, edge thickness and required compression before ordering
  - Allow the seal to reach workshop temperature before installation in cold conditions
- **inquiryChecklist**:
  - Profile shape (U-channel, bulb-edge or custom)
  - Edge thickness or groove size to be sealed
  - Required hardness range or material specification
  - Length per piece and total quantity
  - Application environment (mining, vehicle, marine, etc.)
  - Target color and any surface finish requirements
  - Sample, drawing or existing seal reference

## 中文文案

- **eyebrow**: 密封与边缘防护
- **summary**: 可定制切割的 EPDM 橡胶密封条，用于防水、防风、防尘及边缘保护。
- **description**: 提供 U 型槽和泡型边 EPDM 橡胶密封条，按客户尺寸供货。密封条可在面板、门板、舱口和板材边缘形成柔性密封，具备良好的耐候、抗紫外和耐臭氧性能，适用于室内外环境。
- **featureHeading**: 柔性 EPDM 型材，实现可靠密封。
- **features**:
  - EPDM 橡胶配方，硬度 60–65 邵氏 A
  - U 型槽与泡型边结构，用于面板及边缘密封
  - 具备防水、防风、气密和防尘密封功能
  - 耐紫外线、耐臭氧、耐候性好
  - 尺寸与长度可按需求定制
  - 适用于矿山设备、机械、车辆及船用密封场景
- **applications**:
  - 矿山与工程机械驾驶室及舱室密封
  - 工业设备门、舱口和检修口密封
  - 商用车与货车厢体密封
  - 船舶甲板、舷窗和舱门密封
  - 通用橡胶密封与边缘防护
- **precautions**:
  - 存放于阴凉、干燥处，避免阳光直射、臭氧源和溶剂接触
  - 避免锐角弯折、扭结以及与可使 EPDM 老化的油脂接触
  - 使用锋利刀具裁切，保持切口整齐
  - 订货前确认沟槽尺寸、板边厚度和所需压缩量
  - 低温环境下安装前让密封条恢复至车间温度
- **inquiryChecklist**:
  - 截面形状（U 型槽、泡型边或定制）
  - 待密封板边厚度或沟槽尺寸
  - 所需硬度范围或材料规格
  - 单件长度及总数量
  - 应用环境（矿山、车辆、船用等）
  - 目标颜色及表面要求
  - 样品、图纸或现有密封条参考

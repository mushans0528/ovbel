## 3. 应用模块

### English

**Section label:** Applications

**Heading:** Built for stable conveying on an incline.

**Body:** Chevron conveyor belts are used to convey powdery, granular and block materials where a smooth belt may allow the load to slide or roll back. The source product information gives a reference inclination range of 0–40°. Final suitability depends on the material, moisture, particle size, belt speed, loading method and selected profile.

**Application tags:**

- Mining and aggregate handling
- Grain and agricultural products
- Powder and granular materials
- Bagged and packaged goods
- General bulk-material conveying

### 简体中文

**栏目标签：** 应用场景

**标题：** 为倾斜工况下的稳定输送而设计。

**正文：** 当光面输送带可能导致物料下滑或回滚时，花纹输送带可用于输送粉状、颗粒状和块状物料。原产品资料给出的参考倾角为 0–40°。最终适用性取决于物料、含水率、粒径、带速、装载方式和所选花纹。

**应用标签：**

- 矿山与砂石骨料
- 粮食与农产品
- 粉状与颗粒物料
- 袋装与包装货物
- 通用散料输送

## 4. 特点模块

### English

**Section label:** Product features

**Heading:** Profiles matched to the conveying duty.

1. Anti-slip raised profiles improve material grip on inclined conveyors.
2. Profiles and the top cover are vulcanized as an integral construction.
3. Profile shape, angle and spacing can be selected around the application.
4. The patterned surface supports effective drainage.
5. Custom profile designs are available for suitable projects.

### 简体中文

**栏目标签：** 产品特点

**标题：** 围绕输送工况匹配花纹结构。

1. 凸起防滑花纹增强倾斜输送时的物料抓附。
2. 花纹与上覆盖胶采用整体硫化结构。
3. 可根据应用选择花纹形状、角度和间距。
4. 花纹表面具有良好的排水性能。
5. 合适的项目支持定制花纹设计。

## 7. 储运模块

### English

**Heading:** Handling and storage

- Keep the belt clean during transport and storage.
- Protect it from direct sunlight, rain and snow.
- Avoid contact with acids, alkalis, oils and organic solvents.
- Store the belt at least one metre away from heating equipment.
- Follow the confirmed compound-specific technical and safety instructions when supplied.

### 简体中文

**标题：** 运输与储存

- 运输和储存期间保持输送带清洁。
- 避免阳光直射以及雨雪侵袭。

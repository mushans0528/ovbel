## 2. 首屏文案

### English

**H1:** Polyester Core Conveyor Belt

**Lead:** Multi-ply polyester canvas core rubber belting for ambient-temperature conveying of powder, granular and small-block materials.

**Description:** A multi-ply polyester canvas carcass bonded with high-strength, wear-resistant flexible rubber covers on the top and bottom, designed for the transmission of small and medium block, powder and granular materials without special corrosion requirements.

**Primary CTA:** Request a quote

**Secondary CTA:** Ask a technical question

### 简体中文

**H1：** 聚酯芯输送带

**摘要：** 以多层聚酯帆布为带芯的橡胶输送带，适用于常温粉状、中小颗粒与块状物料输送。

**说明：** 聚酯芯输送带由多层聚酯帆布经特定方式粘合，上下覆盖高强度、耐磨柔性橡胶，适用于无特殊腐蚀要求的中小型块状、粉状和粒状物料输送。

## 6. 储运模块

### English

**Heading:** Handling and storage

- Keep the belt clean during transport and storage.
- Protect it from direct sunlight, rain and snow.
- Avoid contact with acids, alkalis, oils and organic solvents.
- Store the belt at least one metre away from heating equipment.
- Follow the confirmed compound-specific technical and safety instructions when supplied.

### 简体中文

**标题：** 运输与储存

- 运输和储存期间保持输送带清洁。
- 避免阳光直射以及雨雪侵袭。
- 避免接触酸、碱、油和有机溶剂。

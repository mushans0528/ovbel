  - Black general-purpose rubber sheet for industrial and technical use
  - Supplied in standard sheets or cut-to-size on request
  - Standard thickness range 1.0 mm to 100.0 mm
  - Reference SBR / NR-SBR compound with defined physical properties
  - Suitable for gaskets, pads, linings, seals and fabrication work
- **applications**：
  - Gasket and seal fabrication
  - Workshop, bench and equipment lining
  - Impact pads and vibration-damping mats
  - General industrial maintenance and repair
  - Technical rubber parts and protective covers
- **specifications**：
  - Color：Black
  - Reference polymer：NR / SBR rubber compound
  - Standard thickness range：1.0 mm to 100.0 mm
  - Dimensions：Standard sheet or cut-to-size on customer's request
  - Tensile strength：2.5 MPa (ISO 37-2011)
  - Elongation at break：180% (ISO 37-2011)

  - Confirm polymer grade, hardness, thickness and dimensions before ordering
  - Allow the sheet to reach workshop temperature before cutting or bonding in cold conditions

### 2.1 结构（Structure，原文）
> "It uses the new type steel cable as the tension element. On one side or two sides vertical that is perpendicular to the lengthways steel cable, evenly lay the reinforcing layer of the steel cable, steel."
> "Steel Cord Conveyor Belt has High tensile strength, low extensibility, small used transmission pulley diameter, uniform steel wire rope tension, excellent troughbility and good adhesive between rubber and steel wire."

要点提炼：
- 新型钢丝绳为张力元件；
- 平行于纵向钢丝绳的单侧或双侧均匀铺设横向钢丝增强层；
- 高拉伸强度、低伸长率、可用较小传动滚筒直径、钢丝绳张力均匀、成槽性优异、橡胶与钢丝绳粘合良好。

## 三、编辑性改写（围绕已确认事实重新撰写，不照搬原句）

### 3.1 description（英文）
"A new-type steel cable serves as the tension element, with transverse steel reinforcing layers laid perpendicular to the longitudinal cables on one or both sides. The construction provides high tensile strength, low extensibility, small transmission pulley diameters, uniform cable tension, excellent troughability and good adhesion between rubber and steel wire."

### 3.2 features（英文，6 条）
- New-type steel cable tension elements with transverse steel reinforcement
- High tensile strength supports heavy-load transportation
- Low extensibility maintains stable running over long distances
- Small transmission pulley diameters reduce system footprint
- Uniform steel wire tension and good rubber-to-steel adhesion
- Excellent troughability for stable material carrying

### 3.3 中文版（围绕事实重写）
- 描述：采用新型钢丝绳作为张力元件，在平行于纵向钢丝绳的一侧或两侧均匀铺设横向钢丝增强层。该结构具有高拉伸强度、低伸长率、可用较小传动滚筒直径、钢丝绳张力均匀、成槽性优异及橡胶与钢丝绳粘合良好等特性。
- 应用：煤矿开采与输送 / 矿石港口与码头 / 冶金行业 / 电力行业 / 化工行业。

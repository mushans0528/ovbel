# 02 — 双语页面文案

## 英文文案

- **eyebrow**: Vertical material handling
- **summary**: A broad bucket range for grain, food, agricultural and industrial elevator systems.
- **description**: Over 12 bucket models and nearly 400 size references are offered in mild steel, stainless steel, HDPE, nylon 6, reinforced nylon and PU. Buckets can be matched to material abrasiveness, temperature and food-contact requirements, with mounting-hole spacing and dimensions confirmed against the elevator layout.
- **applicationIntro**: Elevator buckets are used in vertical conveying systems where material is scooped and discharged at height. Material selection depends on the conveyed product, temperature and whether food-contact approval is required. The recommendations below are taken from the source page; final model, size and spacing must be confirmed before quotation.
- **featureHeading**: Material and model variety for vertical conveying duties.
- **features**:
  - Six material options: mild steel, stainless steel, HDPE, nylon 6, reinforced nylon and PU
  - Over 12 bucket models and nearly 400 size references
  - AA, DM, EU and other mounting styles available
  - Mounting holes customizable for non-DM/EU bucket types
  - DM type spacing recommended at C+2 mm; other types at C+10 mm
  - Nylon and PU bucket sizes are approximately 2% larger than equivalent HDPE sizes
  - Engineering capacity based on water level + 10%
- **applications**: Grain elevators and feed handling, Food processing (FDA-approved material options available), Industrial bulk material handling, High-temperature material elevation (mild and stainless steel), Abrasive, sharp and sticky materials (nylon and PU options)
- **precautions**:
  - Store buckets in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight, oils and solvents
  - Handle plastic buckets to avoid impact deformation; stack within recommended limits
  - Confirm model, material, dimensions and mounting-hole spacing before ordering
  - Select material to match conveyed product, temperature and abrasion duty
  - Actual bucket spacing may be adjusted in the field based on elevator design
- **inquiryChecklist**: Bucket model or type (e.g., AA, DM, EU), Required material and working temperature, Bucket dimensions (A, B, C, H, T) or size reference, Mounting hole spacing and hole diameter, Quantity and destination port, Application material and temperature

## 中文文案

- **eyebrow**: 垂直物料输送
- **summary**: 适用于粮食、食品、农业和工业提升系统的多系列料斗。
- **description**: 提升机料斗提供低碳钢、不锈钢、HDPE、尼龙 6、增强尼龙和 PU 等六种材料选择，拥有 12 个以上料斗系列、近 400 个尺寸参考，可针对不同物料磨蚀性、温度和食品接触要求进行匹配。安装孔间距与具体尺寸需结合提升机布局确认。
- **applicationIntro**: 提升机料斗用于垂直输送系统，将物料舀取后提升至高处卸料。材料选择取决于输送物料、工作温度以及是否需要食品接触认证。以下建议摘录自原页面；最终型号、尺寸与间距需在报价前确认。
- **featureHeading**: 材料与型号多样，满足垂直输送工况。
- **features**:
  - 六种材料可选：低碳钢、不锈钢、HDPE、尼龙 6、增强尼龙、PU
  - 12 个以上料斗系列、近 400 个尺寸参考
  - 可提供 AA、DM、EU 等多种安装结构
  - 非 DM/EU 型料斗支持定制安装孔
  - DM 型料斗推荐间距 C+2 mm，其余类型 C+10 mm
  - 尼龙和 PU 料斗尺寸较同规格 HDPE 料斗约大 2%
  - 工程设计容量按满水容积 + 10% 参考
- **applications**: 粮食提升机与饲料输送, 食品加工（可选 FDA 食品接触认证材料）, 工业散料垂直输送, 高温物料提升（低碳钢、不锈钢）, 磨蚀性、尖锐及粘性物料（尼龙、PU）
- **precautions**:
  - 料斗应存放于清洁、干燥环境，避免阳光直射及接触油类、溶剂
  - 塑料料斗搬运时应避免撞击变形，堆码不超过建议高度
  - 订货前确认型号、材料、尺寸及安装孔间距
  - 根据输送物料、温度和磨蚀工况选择合适材料
  - 现场安装时可根据提升机设计适当调整料斗间距
- **inquiryChecklist**: 料斗型号或结构类型（如 AA、DM、EU）, 所需材料及工作温度, 料斗尺寸（A、B、C、H、T）或尺寸编号, 安装孔间距及孔径, 数量、包装及目的港, 应用物料及温度

## 2. 简介（description）

**EN**
An endless conveyor belt is produced as a continuous loop with no joints, eliminating the weak point where splices would fail and giving a longer service life. The smooth surface and uniform tension deliver stable operation with minimal elongation in use. It is available in two models — high-strength and standard — and is built from specially impregnated and moulded materials that provide exceptional ply adhesion. The carcass uses high-tensile nylon, polyester or cotton canvas, while the durable cover rubber offers superior elasticity, impact resistance, tear resistance and abrasion performance. Ideal for medium and small belt conveyors, it needs no on-site splicing: simply fit the belt onto the rollers and it is ready to run.

**ZH**
环形输送带为无接头连续环形结构，消除了接头处的薄弱点，使用寿命更长。表面平整、张力均匀，运行中平稳且伸长率小。提供高强度型与标准型两种型号，采用特殊浸渍与模压工艺，层间粘合强度高。带芯可选用高强尼龙、聚酯或棉帆布，覆盖胶弹性好、抗冲击、抗撕裂、耐磨性能优异。适用于中小型带式输送机，无需现场接头，套装在托辊上即可运行，节省停机时间。

## 3. 应用引导（applicationIntro）

**EN** — Endless conveyor belts suit medium-distance, medium-load conveying of powder, granular and small-block materials, and are widely used in mining, coal, ports, metallurgy, chemical, construction and power stations.
**ZH** — 环形输送带适用于中等距离、中等负荷的粉状、粒状及小块物料输送，广泛用于矿山、煤矿、港口、冶金、化工、建材与电站等场合。

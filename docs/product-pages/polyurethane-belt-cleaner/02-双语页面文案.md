## 储运须知（precautions）

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Install on the head pulley at the discharge point, with the blade angle set to peel material away cleanly | 安装于头部滚筒卸料点，调整刮刀角度使物料被剥离干净 |
| Confirm belt width, head pulley diameter and material path width before selecting blade length and segment count | 选型前确认带宽、头部滚筒直径及物料通道宽度，以确定刮刀长度和分段数 |
| Avoid overtightening; excessive pressure accelerates blade and belt wear | 避免过度张紧，过大压力会加速刮刀和皮带磨损 |
| Inspect blade wear periodically and replace segments before the metal support contacts the belt | 定期检查刮刀磨损，在金属支撑接触皮带前更换刮片段 |
| Store blades and hardware in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight, oils and solvents | 刮刀及配件应存放于清洁、干燥、避免阳光直射及远离油类、溶剂的环境 |

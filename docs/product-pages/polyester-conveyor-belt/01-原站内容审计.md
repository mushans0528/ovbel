## 二、已确认事实（来自源页，可直接使用）

### 2.1 产品定位（产品描述，原文）
> "Polyester core conveyer belt is a multi-layer polyester canvas bonded together by a certain way, from top to bottom coverage of high strength, good wear-resistant flexible rubber, no special corrosion for the transmission of small and medium-sized block, powder, granular and other materials."

要点提炼：
- 多层聚酯帆布带芯；
- 上下覆盖高强度、耐磨、柔韧橡胶；
- 用于输送中小块状、粉状、颗粒状等**无特殊腐蚀要求**的物料。

### 2.2 标准清单（Standards，原文）
> "GB/T7984-2001, DIN22102, BS490, AS1332, RMA, JISK6322, SABS1173 etc"

### 2.3 特性（Features，原文 2 点）
1. "Good flexibility, groove formation property and cost-effective."
2. "It is mainly used to transport powder, small and medium granular or less wearing materials at common temperature."

### 2.4 应用（Application，原文 2 点）
1. "Applicable to long-distance, high capacity, high-speed delivery of materials under the conditions."
2. "Used in mining, quarries, chemical plants, architectural industry and etc."

## 三、编辑性改写（围绕已确认事实重新撰写，不照搬原句）

### 3.1 description（英文）
"A multi-ply polyester canvas core is bonded together and covered top and bottom with high-strength, wear-resistant, flexible rubber. It conveys small and medium blocks, powder, granular and other materials where no special corrosion resistance is required, offering good flexibility, good trough formation and cost-effective operation."

### 3.2 features（英文，6 条）
- Multi-ply polyester canvas core for strength and stability
- High-strength, wear-resistant flexible rubber covers
- Good flexibility and trough formation for stable material carrying
- Suitable for powder, granular and small-to-medium block materials
- Cost-effective choice for common-temperature conveying duties
- Reference standards include GB/T7984, DIN22102, BS490 and AS1332

### 3.3 中文版（围绕事实重写）
- 描述：多层聚酯帆布带芯经粘合后，上下覆盖高强度、耐磨、柔韧的橡胶层。适用于输送中小块状、粉状、颗粒状等无特殊腐蚀要求的物料，具有良好的柔韧性、成槽性与经济性。
- 应用：矿山与采石作业 / 化工厂 / 建筑与建材行业 / 长距离、大运量输送线 / 高速物料输送 / 粉状、颗粒状与小块状物料输送。

### 3.4 specifications（围绕事实）
- 带芯：多层聚酯帆布
- 覆盖胶：高强度、耐磨、柔韧橡胶
- 参照标准：GB/T7984-2001、DIN22102、BS490、AS1332、RMA、JISK6322、SABS1173
- 运行条件：常温下输送粉状、颗粒状及中小块状物料

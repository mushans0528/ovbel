## Precautions
| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Store rolls off the ground in a clean, dry, ventilated place away from direct sunlight | 卷装应离地存放于清洁、干燥、通风处，避免阳光直射 |
| Keep away from heat sources, ozone-generating devices and open flames | 远离热源、臭氧发生装置和明火 |
| Avoid contact with oils, solvents, acids and alkalis that may attack the rubber | 避免接触油品、溶剂、酸碱等可能侵蚀橡胶的化学品 |
| Handle with suitable lifting equipment; do not drop, drag or bend sharply | 使用合适的吊装设备，禁止抛掷、拖拽和剧烈弯折 |
| Check belt tension and pulley alignment during installation and initial running | 安装与初期运行时检查张紧与带轮对中 |

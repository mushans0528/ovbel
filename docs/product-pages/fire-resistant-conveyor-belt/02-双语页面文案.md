# 02 — 双语页面文案

## 英文文案

### Eyebrow
Flame-retardant and static-conductive conveying

### Title / Name
Fire Resistant Conveyor Belt

### Summary
Cotton, nylon or EP canvas conveyor belt with fire-resistant and static-conductive properties for flammable or explosive environments.

### Description
The fire resistant conveyor belt is made of cotton canvas, nylon canvas or EP canvas and finished through calendering, assembling and vulcanizing. It is designed for conveying materials that require flame-resistant and static-conductive belts in power, chemical, light, metallurgical and grain processing industries, especially in flammable or explosive environments above coal mines.

### Application Intro
Fire resistant conveyor belts are selected where the conveyed material or operating atmosphere creates a fire or explosion hazard, such as coal and similar combustible bulk materials above coal mines. Belt grade and cover properties are confirmed against the applicable standard and site conditions before quotation.

### Feature Heading
Flame-retardant cover and conductive carcass for hazardous conveying.

### Features
1. Cotton, nylon or EP fabric carcass options
2. Calendered, assembled and vulcanized construction
3. Fire-resistant cover compound
4. Static-conductive design to reduce electrostatic accumulation
5. Suitable for flammable or explosive environments above coal mines
6. Available to common international flame-retardant standards

### Applications
- Coal mining and coal conveying
- Power generation
- Chemical industry
- Light industry
- Metallurgical industry
- Grain processing and storage

### Specifications
- Carcass: Cotton canvas, nylon canvas or EP canvas
- Process: Calendering, assembling and vulcanizing
- Cover tensile strength: ≥18 MPa / ≥14 MPa depending on grade
- Cover elongation at break: ≥450% / ≥400% depending on grade
- Cover abrasion loss: ≤200 mm³ / ≤250 mm³ depending on grade
- Flame retardant grades: K2 / K3
- Flame duration: Total ≤45 s; average ≤15 s; individual ≤60 s
- Anti-static property: ≤3×10⁸ Ω
- Reappearance of flame: No reappearance of flame on any test piece

### Fire-Resistant Grades / Patterns
- SANS-F — South African flame-resistant grade for materials with fire hazards such as coal.
- IS-1891 (FR Grade) — Indian standard fire-resistant conveyor belt grade.
- ISO-340 (FR Grade) — ISO standard fire-resistant grade for flammable-material conveying.
- AS-F / AS-1332 (FR Grade) — Australian fire-resistant belt grades for above-ground and general use.
- DIN S / DIN K Grade — German standard fire-resistant grades; K grade has higher elongation requirement.
- MSHA-FR — Mine Safety and Health Administration flame-resistant grade for mining applications.
- CAN/CSA (FR) — Canadian standard fire-resistant conveyor belt grade.

### Storage & Handling
- Store coils off the floor in a clean, dry, ventilated place away from direct sunlight.
- Keep away from heat sources, ozone-generating equipment and open flames.
- Avoid contact with oils, solvents, acids, alkalis or other chemicals that may attack rubber.
- Handle with suitable lifting equipment; do not throw, drag or bend sharply.
- Ensure splices, pulleys and take-up are correctly installed before initial operation.

### Inquiry Checklist
- Conveyed material, bulk density and fire hazard classification
- Applicable flame-retardant standard or grade required
- Required belt width, length and carcass type
- Cover grade and thickness requirements
- Operating temperature and environmental conditions
- Quantity required and destination port

## 中文文案

### 眉标
阻燃、抗静电的织物芯输送带

### 标题 / 名称
阻燃输送带

### 摘要
以棉帆布、尼龙帆布或 EP 帆布为带芯，经压延、成型、硫化等工艺制成，适用于易燃、易爆环境中需要阻燃和导静电性能的物料输送。

### 详情描述
阻燃输送带采用棉帆布、尼龙帆布或 EP 帆布为带芯，经过压延、成型和硫化工艺完成。产品适用于电力、化工、轻工、冶金和粮食加工等行业，以及煤矿井上和其他易燃、易爆环境中需要阻燃、导静电输送带的场合。

### 应用说明
阻燃输送带用于输送物料或运行环境存在燃烧、爆炸风险的场所，例如煤矿井上输送煤炭及其他可燃散料。阻燃等级、覆盖胶性能需根据适用标准和现场工况在报价前确认。

### 优势标题
阻燃覆盖胶与导静电结构，满足危险场所输送需求。

### 优势
1. 可选棉帆布、尼龙帆布或 EP 织物带芯
2. 经压延、成型、硫化工艺制成
3. 具有阻燃性能的覆盖胶
4. 导静电设计，减少静电积聚
5. 适用于煤矿井上及其他易燃易爆环境
6. 可满足常用国际阻燃标准要求

### 应用行业
- 煤矿及煤炭输送
- 电力行业
- 化工行业
- 轻工行业
- 冶金行业
- 粮食加工与仓储

### 规格参数
- 带芯：棉帆布、尼龙帆布或 EP 帆布
- 工艺：压延、成型、硫化
- 覆盖胶拉伸强度：≥18 MPa / ≥14 MPa（视等级而定）
- 覆盖胶拉断伸长率：≥450% / ≥400%（视等级而定）
- 覆盖胶磨耗量：≤200 mm³ / ≤250 mm³（视等级而定）
- 阻燃等级：K2 / K3
- 火焰持续时间：总值 ≤45 s；平均值 ≤15 s；单值 ≤60 s
- 抗静电性能：≤3×10⁸ Ω
- 火焰复现：任何试件均不得出现火焰复现

### 阻燃等级 / 型号
- SANS-F：南非阻燃等级，适用于煤炭等存在火灾危险的物料输送。
- IS-1891（FR 等级）：印度标准阻燃输送带等级。
- ISO-340（FR 等级）：ISO 标准阻燃等级，用于可燃物料输送。
- AS-F / AS-1332（FR 等级）：澳大利亚阻燃输送带等级，适用于地面及一般用途。
- DIN S / DIN K 等级：德国标准阻燃等级；K 等级对伸长率要求更高。
- MSHA-FR：美国矿山安全与健康管理局阻燃等级，用于矿山应用。
- CAN/CSA（FR）：加拿大标准阻燃输送带等级。

### 储运须知
- 卷装应离地存放于清洁、干燥、通风处，避免阳光直射。
- 远离热源、臭氧发生装置和明火。
- 避免接触油品、溶剂、酸碱等可能侵蚀橡胶的化学品。
- 使用合适的吊装设备，禁止抛掷、拖拽和剧烈弯折。
- 安装与初期运行前检查接头、带轮与张紧装置。

- 覆盖胶等级与厚度要求
- 运行温度与环境条件
- 需求量与目的港
